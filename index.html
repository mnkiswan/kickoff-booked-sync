The problem

The calendar and the CRM disagreed

A kickoff could be booked on the calendar while the CRM record stayed out of date, so the team lost track of when the client was meeting.

How it works

From trigger to finished work

Trigger: an appointment is booked in Zoho Bookings.

01

Appointment booked

Zoho Bookings sends the booking to the flow.

02

Fetch the contact

The matching contact is looked up in Zoho CRM.

03

Update the CRM record

The CRM module entry is updated with the booking details.

Why it matters for the business

What changes when this runs

CRM matches the calendar

Sales and delivery see the kickoff date in the CRM without checking the calendar.

No manual logging

Nobody has to copy booking details into the CRM.

Better handoffs

The record is ready before the kickoff call starts.
